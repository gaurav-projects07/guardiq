How it works

A straightforward safety workflow.

The intended workflow is simple to understand and designed to fit alongside approved laboratory procedures.

Connect & configure

Check heater compatibility, install using qualified personnel, and set validated temperature limits.

Monitor conditions

Read temperature data and compare it with the configured operating and safety thresholds.

Alert & respond

Notify the responsible team and, with validated hardware, initiate the configured power cut-off response.
