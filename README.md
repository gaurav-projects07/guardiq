# GuardIQ — Smart Heater Safety Controller

**Smarter Monitoring. Safer Laboratory Workflows.**

🌐 **Live Website:** https://gaurav-projects07.github.io/guardiq/

GuardIQ is a smart laboratory safety product concept designed to monitor heating equipment, detect unsafe temperature conditions, and initiate an automatic power cut-off when configured safety limits are reached.

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Laboratory heating equipment can pose serious safety risks when left unattended or when unexpected overheating occurs. Such incidents may endanger researchers, damage expensive equipment, and disrupt critical experiments.

## 💡 Our Solution

GuardIQ proposes an additional layer of protection for laboratory heating equipment through intelligent temperature monitoring and automated safety responses.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Real-Time Temperature Monitoring:** Monitors temperature readings against configured safety thresholds.
- **Automatic Power Cut-Off:** Designed to disconnect power when unsafe conditions are detected, subject to compatible and validated hardware.
- **Safety Alerts:** Helps notify laboratory staff when temperature readings exceed configured limits.
- **Plug-and-Play Vision:** Aims to simplify integration with compatible laboratory heating equipment.
- **Responsive Website:** Designed for desktop and mobile devices.
- **Pilot Enquiry Form:** Helps interested institutions prepare an enquiry about a potential demonstration or pilot.
